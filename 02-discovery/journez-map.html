Driver Future-State Journey

Initiative: removing core-workflow complexity to retain at-risk enterprise accounts

Draft for review. Future-state features are concepts to validate, not committed scope. References (BUG / UXR) point to the source research behind each pain point.

Persona

Experienced delivery driver running a full route, repeating the same few actions dozens of times a day.

Goal

Get through the route fast, using only Start Route, Mark Delivered, and directions that match the real roads.

Friction today

Three screens to mark a stop delivered, then up to three photo retakes because uploads are never confirmed.

Future-state timeline

1
LaunchOne-tap start
User actionTaps Start Route from a core-only home screen → route begins instantly.
Internal stateExpects a menu hunt → finds only what they need, feels in control.
Pain point addressedCore actions buried 2–3 levels deep → no menu navigation. BUG-2079, UXR-11
2
NavigateAccurate, live route
User actionFollows a route that avoids closures and remembers overrides → no daily re-correction.
Internal stateStops second-guessing directions → trusts the app over memory or paper.
Pain point addressedIgnored closures and late reassignments → always on the current route. BUG-2068, BUG-2044
3
DeliverOne-tap proof
User actionOne tap marks delivered and captures the photo → done one-handed at the doorstep.
Internal stateSees a clear “saved” confirmation → no doubt, no retakes, moves on.
Pain point addressedThree-screen flow and silent upload failures → confirmed proof, queued if offline. BUG-2055, BUG-2061
4
SyncAutomatic handoff
User actionStatus reaches dispatch automatically → no texting, no paper tick-off.
Internal stateTrusts the app as the record → leaves the paper manifest behind.
Pain point addressedTexting dispatch and dashboard lag → dispatcher sees live status. UXR-01, BUG-2072

Competitive advantages over the manual workaround

One source of truthEvery delivery recorded once, timestamped and linked to its stop. The workaround leaves the app, text thread, and paper manifest saying different things.
Accurate reporting, protected renewalDriver data flows into the admin reporting customers bought the product for (UXR-10). The workaround quietly erodes it.
Dispatcher time backNo reading texts or re-entering statuses, and reroutes reach drivers immediately: “seconds, not screens” for dispatch.
Notes and assumptions
  • The strategy statement (“human-led curation and restoration over mass-market scale”) has been read as a focused, curated core experience over feature sprawl. Confirm this interpretation.
  • The value proposition targets dispatchers; this journey maps the driver persona and applies the same speed principle, with dispatcher benefit realized in Stage 4.
  • Stage 3’s offline queuing depends on fixing offline stop-list caching (BUG-2050).
  • If this is shared with customers or externally, a human must review it first, and naming a customer or quoting account figures needs clearance against that customer’s contract.
