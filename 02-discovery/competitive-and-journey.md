# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** An experienced delivery driver running a full route, who uses the same few actions dozens of times a day
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** To get through the route fast, using only Start Route, Mark Delivered and directions that match the real roads.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** At a doorstep in the rain with a package in one hand, the driver has to tap through three screens to mark a stop delivered, then retake a proof-of-delivery photo up to three times because the app never confirms the upload went through
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** Texting the dispatcher instead of using Mark Delivered
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** Open a personal messaging app and text the dispatcher that the stop is done (evidenced: UXR-01 says the driver texts dispatch; the message content is inferred).
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** The driver still has to report each stop, just in a tool with no structure. The dispatcher then has to read the text.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** The dispatcher receives the text and either updates the status by hand or doesn’t
