# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** Dispatch coordinators at the enterprise platform and regional fleets that run RouteLogic. They manage many drivers at once from the dispatcher dashboard and handle reassignments and reroutes in real time as the day changes.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Keep every delivery on track by changing a route and knowing right away that the driver has it, so they can run the whole day from one system they trust instead of chasing drivers by phone.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** The dispatcher reroutes a driver but can't see whether it worked, so they stop trusting the system and coordinate with drivers over WhatsApp instead
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** The dispatcher uses WhatsApp groups, phone calls and text messages, all outside RouteLogic.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** 1.The dispatcher reroutes the driver in the RouteLogic dashboard.
2. They post the same change in the WhatsApp group or call the driver, because the reroute takes 8–15 minutes to arrive and the driver gets no notification.
3. They wait for the driver to confirm by message or phone.
4. They track progress through WhatsApp updates, because the dashboard status runs 20–60 minutes behind.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** The moment right after the reroute, when the dashboard can't show whether the driver got it and the dispatcher has to pick up the phone.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** One dispatch team calls its WhatsApp group "the real system."(BUG-2044, BUG-2072, UXR-02, UXR-09).
