## Pre-work · Hypothesis check
- **Role , who you are solving for (from M2):** Dispatch coordinators at enterprise and regional fleets that use RouteLogic. From the dispatcher dashboard they manage many drivers at once and handle reassignments and reroutes in real time as the day changes.
- **Goal , what this user is ultimately trying to achieve:** Keep every delivery on track. They want to change a route and know right away that the driver has it, so they can run the whole day from one system they trust instead of chasing drivers by phone.
- **Friction / moment of misery , the specific pain blocking their goal:** The moment right after a reroute. The dispatcher sends a change in RouteLogic but can't tell whether the driver received it. Because they can't confirm it worked, they stop trusting the system
- **Current workaround , the external tool or manual process they rely on (M2):** WhatsApp groups, phone calls and text messages, all outside RouteLogic. After every reroute in the dashboard, the dispatcher posts the same change in WhatsApp or calls the driver, waits for the driver to confirm, and then tracks progress through WhatsApp updates instead of the dashboard.
- **Problem Hook , your one-sentence framing of the business crisis (M1):** We must prevent our largest customer from switching to a leaner rival by removing the UI/UX complexity that makes simple reroutes slow and frustrating.
- **Value Proposition , the outcome your initiative promised to deliver (M1):** For fleet dispatchers who make split-second decisions every day, RouteLogic should provide a fast, focused workspace where urgent tasks like rerouting a driver take seconds, not several screens of configurations, because the people running live operations need maximum speed and clarity

## Step 3 · Craft your hypothesis
- **Qualitative evidence (from M2) , quote the specific friction / moment of misery for your persona:** Right after a reroute, coordinators can't see whether the driver received it, so they confirm by WhatsApp or phone.
- **Quantitative evidence (from M3) , name the metric or data point that confirms the pain; cite the number:** Coordinators lose 31 min/day to manual workarounds, up from ~9 (3.4x). Coordinator NPS fell from +18 to −12, and 4 of 5 churned accounts cite complexity.
- **Persona , role, goal, and the friction you confirmed in the reconciliation steps:** Dispatch coordinators at enterprise and regional fleets who reroute many drivers in real time.
- **Problem you are solving , one sentence describing the specific friction this initiative removes:** Coordinators have no way to confirm in RouteLogic that a reroute reached the driver, which forces them into WhatsApp and phone calls.
- **Strategic outcome , what behaviour change do you expect, and how does it map to retention / revenue / churn?:** Coordinators confirm reroutes in RouteLogic and stop double-handling them in WhatsApp. That rebuilds trust and NPS and lowers the churn risk.
- **Primary success metric (initiative signal) , the leading indicator that tells you the gap is closing:** Share of reroutes acknowledged by the driver in RouteLogic within 2 minutes.
- **Guardrail metric (product signal) , the metric that must NOT drop; it protects your existing base:** Coordinator CSAT for Core Dispatch must not drop below 4.1.
- **Decision window , how much time or data before you scale, pivot, or kill? minimum threshold to proceed?:** An 8-week pilot with the largest customer. Scale if 80% of reroutes are acknowledged within 2 minutes and workaround time falls to 20 min/day.
