# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If we do nothing, we risk losing our daily users, then our largest customer, and eventually our market position to a simpler competitor that could become the new standard for our domain.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** I imageine the following scenario: A driver calls in stuck in traffic with six stops left. The dispatcher needs to reroute in seconds, but RouteLogic takes several screens and mandatory fields to make the change. So they log in to Google Sheets and update this route because it is faster to so in a separate application.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We must prevent our largest customer from switching to a leaner rival by removing the UI/UX complexity that makes simple reroutes slow and frustrating.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** For fleet dispatchers who make split-second decisions every day, RouteLogic should provide a fast, focused workspace where urgent tasks like rerouting a driver take seconds, not several screens of configurations, because the people running live operations need maximum speed and clarity
